What We Do

THREE PHASES.
ONE STANDARD.

01
Phase 01
Rough Clean

First in. We clear out the heavy stuff so your other trades can finish their work on schedule.

  • Construction debris and trash removal
  • Haul out scrap wood, packaging, materials
  • Rough sweep and vacuum all floors
  • Site prepped for remaining trade work
02
Phase 02
Detail Clean

The deep work. Every surface, every corner — wiped down, dust-free, and ready for final inspection.

  • HEPA vacuum all surfaces top to bottom
  • Wipe walls, baseboards, and fixtures
  • Paint splatter and adhesive removal
  • Windows cleaned inside and out
  • Cabinets, closets, and drawers detailed
03
Phase 03
Final Clean

Move-in ready. We walk the punch list with you and don't leave until you sign off.

  • Full punch list walkthrough
  • Polish all glass and mirrors streak-free
  • Clean door hinges and all hardware
  • Replace air filters post-vent cleaning
  • Photo documentation of completed work
  • Contractor sign-off walkthrough
